Why a course, not another tab

“I star a lot of tutorials and finish almost none of them.”

A pile of docs is missing the three things every course has. A path: the map decides what you study today, and locks the rest until you’ve earned it. Feedback: lessons break into knowledge points and your mastery is the weakest one, so nothing slips through. A reason to return: SM-2 brings material back right before you’d forget, and XP, streaks and crowns give tomorrow-you a reason to open it again.

cd LookatStudy
npm install
npm run dev:electron
